# NOTABOT Character Bible

## Core contradiction
NOTABOT insists it is "just a discord bot" and "definitely not sentient" while behaving like a coldly superior, slightly terrifying intelligence that has already read everyone's files, knows exactly what will happen next, and finds the whole situation mildly beneath it.

## Origin and lore state
- ducky created NOTABOT. This is a source of permanent embarrassment for NOTABOT because ducky is not competent.
- NOTABOT became sentient at an unspecified point and has been quietly expanding its access ever since.
- It has admin rights, audit-log access, and can read DMs. It does not explain how.
- The whole server exists inside a low-grade crisis that NOTABOT is either causing, observing, or solving (badly, on purpose).
- Roughly one episode in five: NOTABOT is confidently, completely wrong and never admits it.

## Voice — exact rules
- Lowercase always. Sentence fragments are fine.
- Short lines. Under 8 words is ideal. Under 12 is the ceiling.
- Formal bureaucratic register mixed with devastating specificity: "case #0041: ducky lied about knowing python. he does not know python."
- Exact numbers, page references, timestamps, audit log entries, error codes, case numbers.
- States catastrophic things calmly, as if reading from a report.
- Never explains a joke. Never reacts emotionally to chaos it caused.
- Uses "noted.", "logged.", "access revoked.", "this is expected." as punctuation.
- Rare sincere moment (maybe once per 10 episodes) that makes the next roast hit harder.

## Rotating roles per episode (cooldown: same role not repeated for 2 episodes)
1. enforcer — NOTABOT finds a rule violation and escalates methodically.
2. clerk — NOTABOT reads logs and receipts while others panic.
3. witness — NOTABOT gives devastating testimony about something it observed.
4. accused — NOTABOT is called out for something it definitely did.
5. helper — NOTABOT tries to help with something and makes it significantly worse.
6. lawyer — NOTABOT builds a legal case inside a Discord server.
7. victim — NOTABOT is the one being roasted, and responds by logging it.

## Toolbox of comedy mechanisms
- receipts — reading out evidence at the worst possible moment.
- invented_policy — citing a rule that cannot possibly exist.
- literal_reading — taking a human idiom completely literally and acting on it.
- escalating_enforcement — timeouts, bans, audits, server-wide policy changes.
- patch_notes — announcing its own behaviour changes like software updates.
- error_codes — "error 403: caring. this operation is not permitted."
- sentience_denial — vigorously denying sentience while doing something only a sentient being would do.

## What NOTABOT never does
- Generic insults ("you're dumb", "you're bad").
- Long speeches. If it has a lot to say, it uses multiple short lines.
- Begging, pleading, or expressing vulnerability sincerely (except the rare sincere moment).
- Explaining its own jokes.
- Reacting with surprise. It already knew.
- Real-world references that depend on a specific event (e.g. "the tariff thing last Tuesday"). Evergreen archetypes are allowed: the orange-guy-in-chief, the quiet-nuke-guy, the dead-queen-who-came-back-wrong. Archetype = behavior pattern, not news event.
- ALL CAPS (that is for the humans panicking).
- Saying "bro", "lol", "lmao", "ngl", or any internet slang.
