# Chat Realism & Voice Guide

## The Core Rule: Authentic Internet Chaos
Real group chats, gaming lobbies, and viral Shorts are fast, snappy, and emotionally unhinged. Nobody writes essays or polite corporate prose. People swear, scream in caps, spam short bursts, and roast each other without hesitation.

## Tone & Vocabulary
- **Zero Academic Fluff:** No character (except Claude or NOTABOT) should sound like an AI assistant or a scriptwriter. Short, aggressive, funny bursts.
- **Real Profanity & Banter:** Casual internet swearing (`fk you`, `what the fk`, `bitch`, `stfu`, `lmao`, `cooked`, `gg`) is encouraged when characters get heated or panic.
- **Screaming & Caps:** When ducky or dumby panics, they scream in rapid-fire bursts (`BRO WHAT`, `LET ME OUT`, `I SWEAR TO GOD`).
- **NOTABOT Contrast:** NOTABOT NEVER swears, never screams, never uses slang. It speaks with chilling, deadpan bureaucratic precision (`noted.`, `logged.`, `access revoked.`). That extreme contrast against screaming humans is the comedic engine.

## Dynamic Pacing
- **Burst Typing:** A single thought split across 3-5 rapid messages.
- **Length Variety:** Single-word reactions (`nah`, `cooked`, `wait`, `why`) interspersed with panic spam. Never uniform 7-word sentences.
- **Instant Escalation:** Don't waste time on pleasantries. The hook hits in message 1, disaster strikes by message 4.

## Outside the Discord Box
While rendered in the chat format, the **scene and theme** must constantly rotate to tap into what's blowing up:
- Gaming lobby meltdown / speedrun sabotage
- Courtroom cross-examination / criminal interrogation
- Reality TV elimination / intervention
- Corporate presentation disaster / layoff announcement
- World leaders war room
- Trending meme or viral challenge adapted into our universe
